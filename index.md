<section id="about" markdown="1">

I'm a researcher at the [Barcelona Supercomputing Center](https://www.bsc.es/), where I build AI methods for research in the social sciences and humanities (SSH). My work sits where data, AI, and the disciplines that use them meet—and I work to make that intersection trustworthy. That commitment runs across three fronts. In research, I develop AI methods for SSH at the BSC, and I'm currently focused on applying trustworthy AI to legislation and public administration. In data standards and data-sharing practices, I'm working on [Croissant](https://mlcommons.org/working-groups/data/croissant/), an MLCommons metadata standard for ML-ready datasets, where I lead the Responsible AI group. And in policy, I was recently appointed by the European Commission to the AI Act Advisory Forum, helping shape how AI is governed across the EU. Before this, I taught web and software engineering courses at UAB and UOC universities, and worked as a software architect for international companies. I also served as a Member of the  [Catalan Parliament](https://www.parlament.cat/web/composicio/diputats-fitxa/index.html?p_codi=1851&p_legislatura=11), serving as spokesperson for its Science and Technology Committee.
</section>
