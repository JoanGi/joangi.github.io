About Projects Publications Community & Teaching Past Experience

I’m a researcher at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center, where I build AI methods for research in the social sciences and humanities (SSH). My work sits where data, AI, and the disciplines that use them meet—and I work to make that intersection trustworthy. That commitment runs across three fronts. In research, I develop AI methods for SSH at the BSC, and I’m currently focused on applying trustworthy AI to legislation and public administration. In data standards and data-sharing practices, I’m working on Croissant, an MLCommons metadata standard for ML-ready datasets, where I lead the Responsible AI group. And in policy, I was recently appointed by the European Commission to the AI Act Advisory Forum, helping shape how AI is governed across the EU. Before this, I taught web and software engineering courses at UAB and UOC universities, and worked as a software architect for international companies. I also served as a Member of the Catalan Parliament, serving as spokesperson for its Science and Technology Committee.
